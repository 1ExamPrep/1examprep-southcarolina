How does it fit the South Carolina Boiler Installation exam?

The Boiler Installation classification covers installing and repairing commercial boilers, piping and controls under the CLB. Boiler work in South Carolina also falls under the state boiler safety program, which the Contractor's Licensing Board oversees, so the exam leans heavily on the ASME codes and safe operating limits.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Boiler Installation technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
