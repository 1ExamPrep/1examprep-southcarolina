How does it fit the South Carolina Air Conditioning exam?

The Air Conditioning classification covers commercial cooling systems, ductwork and controls. Air conditioning candidates work with the mechanical code, duct design and refrigerant handling, and the exam rewards fast lookups in tabbed code books.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Air Conditioning technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)

What will you study with it?

Manual N, Manual D, and Manual J address system design and calculation concepts. Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning provides a broad technical foundation, while the Ductulator supports practical airflow and duct-sizing work. Because this is an open-book examination, candidates should become familiar with each reference before test day. Open-book testing does not eliminate the need to study. You must still recognize the subject of a question, determine which book applies, locate the relevant section, and interpret the information within the available testing time. This package is intended for candidates who want the listed physical references together in one product. It does not include an online course, application service, examination registration, testing fees, or licensing services unless those items are specifically stated in a separate package.
