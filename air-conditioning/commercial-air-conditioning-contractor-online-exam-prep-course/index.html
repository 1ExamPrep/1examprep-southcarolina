How does it fit the South Carolina Air Conditioning exam?

The Air Conditioning classification covers commercial cooling systems, ductwork and controls. Air conditioning candidates work with the mechanical code, duct design and refrigerant handling, and the exam rewards fast lookups in tabbed code books.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Air Conditioning technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)

What will you study with it?

Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning provides broad technical coverage, while the Ductulator supports practical duct-sizing and airflow work. The online course gives candidates a more organized way to move through these materials instead of reading several technical books without a clear sequence. It supports subject review, practice-oriented preparation, reference navigation, and a repeatable method for approaching open-book questions. An open-book examination still requires strong preparation. Candidates must identify the subject of a question, decide which reference applies, locate the correct chapter or section, interpret the material, and select the best answer while managing the available testing time. The course helps build those habits through organized study and practical reference use. This product includes the online exam prep course. The reference books and Ductulator are sold separately unless a selected package specifically states that the books are included.
