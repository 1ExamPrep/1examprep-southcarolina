How does it fit the South Carolina Wood Frame Structures exam?

The Wood Frame Structures classification covers commercial wood-frame buildings. Expect code questions on wood design values, fasteners, sheathing and fire blocking, plus estimating and safety.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Wood Frame Structures technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
