What does the South Carolina Wood Frame Structures license cover?

The Wood Frame Structures classification covers commercial wood-frame buildings. Expect code questions on wood design values, fasteners, sheathing and fire blocking, plus estimating and safety.

On a CLB license the classification shows as a 2-letter code followed by a group number, so WF3 means Wood Frame Structures work up to $1,000,000 per job. A license is needed once regulated commercial work costs more than $10,000.

Which exams do Wood Frame Structures candidates take?

PSI gives every South Carolina contractor exam at its test centers. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current LLR pages and PSI bulletins.

How much work can you bid with the license?

South Carolina general contractor licenses come in groups set by your financial statement or surety bond. The group caps the value of any single job or bid. The initial license fee is $350, and general licenses expire on October 31 of even-numbered years.

GroupLimit per job
Group 1$100,000
Group 2$400,000
Group 3$1,000,000
Group 4$3,000,000
Group 5Unlimited
