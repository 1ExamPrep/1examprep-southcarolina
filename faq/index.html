FAQ

South Carolina Contractor Exam FAQ

South Carolina contractor exams are open-book PSI tests: the Commercial Business and Law exam has 50 questions in 125 minutes and needs 35 correct, while the residential version needs a scale score of 68. The answers below cover exams, licenses and ordering from us.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Key takeaways

  • All CLB and RBC exams are open book.
  • Commercial Business and Law: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 to pass.
  • Residential Business and Law: scale score 68.
  • Free USPS Media Mail on books.

What should you know about the exams?

  • PSI gives every CLB and RBC exam at test centers across the state.
  • Approved references are allowed in the room, so book editions matter.
  • CLB candidates book without pre-approval; RBC candidates need the Exam Eligibility letter.
  • RBC candidates get 12 months to pass both parts.

Which license do you need?

The value and type of the work decide it. Regulated commercial work over $10,000 needs a Contractor's Licensing Board license in the right classification, such as Building, Roofing or Plumbing. Work on homes needs a Residential Builders Commission license: Residential Builder for general building and remodeling, or Residential Electrical, HVAC or Plumbing for those trades. Individual journeyman and master electricians and journeyman plumbers get their cards from the city or county where they work. Alarm businesses are licensed by the CLB under the Alarm System Business Act, with a Fire Alarm exam and an Alarm Code of Laws exam. The license list groups all 33 licenses we cover by board.

How long should you study?

Most candidates need 4 to 8 weeks. Plan for more if you work full time or if you are taking a trade exam and Business and Law in the same month. A good split is 2 weeks learning the layout of each reference book, 2 to 4 weeks of practice questions, and a final 1 to 2 weeks of timed full-length exams. Because every South Carolina contractor exam is open book, the goal is fast lookups rather than memorizing. On the commercial Business and Law exam you have about 2.5 minutes per question, which is why pre-printed tabs and highlighted books are popular. Read the licensing guide for the full process.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions South Carolina candidates ask us most, checked against LLR and PSI on November 24, 2026.

Are South Carolina contractor exams open book?
Yes. The CLB and RBC trade exams and both Business and Law exams allow the approved reference books.
How many questions are on the Commercial Business and Law exam?
50 questions with a 125-minute limit; 35 correct answers pass.
What score passes the Residential Business and Law exam?
A scale score of 68 is required.
Who needs a CLB license?
Anyone performing regulated commercial general or mechanical work where the total cost is over $10,000.
How long is the RBC Exam Eligibility letter valid?
1 year from the date it is issued. You must pass both exam parts within that time or reapply.
Does South Carolina accept the NASCLA exam?
Yes, for the Unlimited Building classification, and a NASCLA pass within 3 years waives the Residential Builder trade exam.
