How does it fit the South Carolina Residential HVAC exam?

A Residential HVAC license from the Residential Builders Commission covers heating, air conditioning and ventilation work on homes. Residential HVAC candidates pass the Residential HVAC exam and the Business Management and Law exam within 12 months of their Exam Eligibility letter.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Residential HVAC exam: Trade knowledge and the residential code (Open book)
  • Business Management and Law for Residential Builders: Scale score of 68 to pass (Open book)
