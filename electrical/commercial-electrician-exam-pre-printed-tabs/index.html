How does it fit the South Carolina Electrical exam?

The Electrical classification covers commercial electrical contracting under the CLB. Electrical contractor candidates face NEC code lookups and calculations; the NASCLA electrical exam is also accepted for some licenses, so check the bulletin before you buy books.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Electrical technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
