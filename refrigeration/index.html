What does the South Carolina Refrigeration license cover?

The Refrigeration classification covers commercial refrigeration equipment, walk-ins and process cooling. Refrigeration questions cover the refrigeration cycle, piping, charging, safety codes and EPA refrigerant rules.

On a CLB license the classification shows as a 2-letter code followed by a group number, so RG3 means Refrigeration work up to $1,000,000 per job. A license is needed once regulated commercial work costs more than $10,000.

Which exams do Refrigeration candidates take?

PSI gives every South Carolina contractor exam at its test centers. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current LLR pages and PSI bulletins.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
PSI Refrigeration technical examTrade knowledge, codes, estimating and safetyBooks allowed (open book)

How much work can you bid with the license?

South Carolina mechanical contractor licenses come in groups set by your financial statement or surety bond. The group caps the value of any single job or bid. The initial license fee is $350, and mechanical licenses expire on October 31 of odd-numbered years.

GroupLimit per job
Group 1$35,000
Group 2$100,000
Group 3$200,000
Group 4$400,000
Group 5Unlimited
