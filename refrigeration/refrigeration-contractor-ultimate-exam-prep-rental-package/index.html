How does it fit the South Carolina Refrigeration exam?

The Refrigeration classification covers commercial refrigeration equipment, walk-ins and process cooling. Refrigeration questions cover the refrigeration cycle, piping, charging, safety codes and EPA refrigerant rules.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Refrigeration technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
