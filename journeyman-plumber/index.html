What does the South Carolina Journeyman Plumber license cover?

Journeyman plumber cards in South Carolina are issued at the local level and tested on the International Plumbing Code. The study packages here are built on the 2021 International Plumbing Code with tabs and practice exams.

Which exams do Journeyman Plumber candidates take?

Local credentials use exams chosen by the city or county. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current LLR pages and PSI bulletins.

Who issues the license in South Carolina?

South Carolina does not issue a statewide journeyman plumber card. Cities and counties do, and most use a PSI or ICC exam built on a recent code edition. Ask the building department where you work which edition it tests, then pick the matching study guide. The 2 packages here include current and older editions, clearly labelled.
