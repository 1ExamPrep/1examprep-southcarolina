How does it fit the South Carolina Nonstructural Renovation exam?

The Nonstructural Renovation classification covers commercial interior renovation that does not touch structural elements: drywall, ceilings, flooring, millwork and finishes. Since January 1, 2025 the Nonstructural Renovation classification requires passing a PSI technical exam, so the books and courses here are built around that newer test.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Nonstructural Renovation technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
