How to Get a South Carolina Contractor License

To get a South Carolina contractor license, decide whether the CLB or the Residential Builders Commission covers your work, pass the PSI trade exam and the Business Management and Law exam, then apply to LLR with your financials. Commercial work over $10,000 needs a CLB license, and the fee is $350.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Key takeaways

  • CLB license required for regulated commercial work over $10,000.
  • CLB fee: $350.
  • Commercial Business and Law: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 to pass.
  • RBC eligibility letter lasts 1 year.
  • GC licenses expire October 31 in even years; MC in odd years.

What are the steps to a South Carolina contractor license?

  1. Pick the board. Commercial general or mechanical work over $10,000 goes to the Contractor's Licensing Board. Residential building and residential trades go to the Residential Builders Commission.
  2. Choose the classification. Match your work to a classification such as Building, Roofing, Plumbing or Residential Builder.
  3. Pass the PSI exams. Take the trade exam and the matching Business Management and Law exam. Both are open book.
  4. Prepare financials. CLB applicants submit a financial statement or surety bond, which sets the license group.
  5. Apply to LLR. Send the application, exam pass sheets and the $350 fee for a CLB license.

Most candidates finish the exams within 4 to 8 weeks of starting to study. The LLR review then takes a few weeks, depending on how complete the application is.

Do you need a CLB or an RBC license?

The Contractor's Licensing Board regulates general and mechanical contractors under Title 40, Chapter 11 of the SC Code. A CLB license is required once regulated commercial work costs more than $10,000, and it also allows residential work. The Residential Builders Commission licenses builders and specialty contractors who only work on homes. Burglar and fire alarm businesses are licensed by the CLB under the Alarm System Business Act.

Pick by your biggest likely job. A remodeler doing kitchens and baths needs the Residential Builder license. A company bidding a $250,000 retail fit-out needs a CLB Building license in Group 2 or above.

CLB candidates do not need approval to book a PSI exam. RBC candidates apply first and receive an Exam Eligibility letter valid for 1 year.

How do license groups and job limits work?

Each CLB license carries a group from 1 to 5. The group sets the most you can bid or contract on one job. Groups 1 and 2 can use an owner-prepared financial statement or a surety bond; higher groups need reviewed or audited statements.

Can you skip a South Carolina exam?

Sometimes. The CLB has waiver agreements with several states for technical exams, but every waiver applicant still passes the South Carolina Business Management and Law exam. For Residential Builder, a current SC General Contractor or a NASCLA pass from the last 3 years waives the trade exam. Residential electrical and plumbing waivers exist with North Carolina and Mississippi.

When does a South Carolina license expire?

General contractor licenses expire on October 31 of even-numbered years. Mechanical contractor licenses expire on October 31 of odd-numbered years. Burglar and fire alarm licenses for 2026 to 2028 had a renewal deadline of October 29, 2026.

How should you study for an open-book exam?

  • Get the exact edition of every book on the PSI reference list.
  • Tab and highlight the sections that answer most questions, or buy them pre-tabbed.
  • Time yourself: the commercial business exam allows about 2.5 minutes per question.
  • Take full practice exams in the last 2 weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions South Carolina candidates ask us most, checked against LLR and PSI on November 24, 2026.

How long does it take to get a South Carolina contractor license?
Most candidates study 4 to 8 weeks, then wait a few weeks for LLR to review the application. RBC candidates also wait for the Exam Eligibility letter before testing.
How much does a CLB license cost?
The CLB licensure fee is $350, plus PSI exam fees paid to PSI and the cost of a surety bond or financial statement for your group.
Is the South Carolina exam hard?
The exams are open book but timed, so the challenge is finding answers quickly. Tabbed books and practice exams make the biggest difference.
Do I need experience?
Yes. Both boards ask for documented experience in the trade; the application lists what counts and who can verify it.
Can a company hold the license?
Yes. A CLB license is issued to the business entity, with a qualifying party who passed the exams.
What is the NASCLA exam?
The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building is a national exam that South Carolina accepts for an Unlimited Building license.
Do I need a license for work under $10,000?
Not a CLB license for commercial work at or under $10,000, but residential work and local rules can still apply. Check with LLR before you bid.
