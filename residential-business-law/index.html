What does the South Carolina Residential Business and Law license cover?

The Business Management and Law for Residential Builders exam is required for the Residential Builder, Residential Electrical, Residential HVAC and Residential Plumbing licenses. Candidates need a scale score of 68 to pass. The exam is open book and is based on the NASCLA Contractor's Guide for South Carolina Residential Builders.

Which exams do Residential Business and Law candidates take?
