What does the South Carolina Marine license cover?

The Marine classification covers docks, piers, bulkheads, seawalls and other work on or over the water. With more than 180 miles of coastline and the Intracoastal Waterway, marine work is a big part of South Carolina construction, and the exam covers pile driving, timber and concrete marine structures and waterfront safety.

On a CLB license the classification shows as a 2-letter code followed by a group number, so MR3 means Marine work up to $1,000,000 per job. A license is needed once regulated commercial work costs more than $10,000.

How much work can you bid with the license?

South Carolina general contractor licenses come in groups set by your financial statement or surety bond. The group caps the value of any single job or bid. The initial license fee is $350, and general licenses expire on October 31 of even-numbered years.

GroupLimit per job
Group 1$100,000
Group 2$400,000
Group 3$1,000,000
Group 4$3,000,000
Group 5Unlimited
