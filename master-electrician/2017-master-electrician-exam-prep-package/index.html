How does it fit the South Carolina Master Electrician exam?

Master electrician credentials in South Carolina are issued locally, and many electrical contractors also hold the CLB Electrical classification. Master-level exams lean on load calculations, service sizing and code lookups, so the calculations guide and a tabbed NEC are worth the most study time.
