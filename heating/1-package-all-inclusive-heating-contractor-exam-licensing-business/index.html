How does it fit the South Carolina Heating exam?

The Heating classification covers commercial heating systems, furnaces, hydronic systems and fuel gas piping. The heating exam leans on the mechanical and fuel gas codes plus heat loss and venting calculations.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Heating technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
