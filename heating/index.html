What does the South Carolina Heating license cover?

The Heating classification covers commercial heating systems, furnaces, hydronic systems and fuel gas piping. The heating exam leans on the mechanical and fuel gas codes plus heat loss and venting calculations.

On a CLB license the classification shows as a 2-letter code followed by a group number, so HT3 means Heating work up to $1,000,000 per job. A license is needed once regulated commercial work costs more than $10,000.

Which exams do Heating candidates take?

PSI gives every South Carolina contractor exam at its test centers. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current LLR pages and PSI bulletins.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
PSI Heating technical examTrade knowledge, codes, estimating and safetyBooks allowed (open book)
Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to passOpen book

How much work can you bid with the license?

South Carolina mechanical contractor licenses come in groups set by your financial statement or surety bond. The group caps the value of any single job or bid. The initial license fee is $350, and mechanical licenses expire on October 31 of odd-numbered years.

GroupLimit per job
Group 1$35,000
Group 2$100,000
Group 3$200,000
Group 4$400,000
Group 5Unlimited
