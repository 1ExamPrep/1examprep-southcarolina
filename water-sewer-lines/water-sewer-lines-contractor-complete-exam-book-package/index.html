How does it fit the South Carolina Water and Sewer Lines exam?

The Water and Sewer Lines classification covers underground water mains, sanitary sewers, storm drains and service lines. Line work questions cover pipe materials, bedding, testing, trench protection and the specifications water and sewer utilities use.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Water and Sewer Lines technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)

What will you study with it?

The DIPRA guide addresses ductile iron pipe handling and installation. The concrete pipe reference supports preparation involving concrete pipe and box culverts. The excavation handbook covers earthwork, grading, measurements, quantities, equipment, and field procedures. The South Carolina 811 manual addresses excavation notification and underground facility damage prevention. Studying with the full reference set also helps candidates develop open-book testing skills. Candidates should learn which book applies to each subject, how every reference is organized, and which terms are most useful when searching an index or table of contents. Familiarity with the books can reduce the time spent searching and help candidates evaluate answers in the context of the applicable procedure or requirement.
