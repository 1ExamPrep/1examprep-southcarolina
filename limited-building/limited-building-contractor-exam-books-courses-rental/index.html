How does it fit the South Carolina Limited Building exam?

The Limited Building (LB) exam covers commercial building work up to 3 stories in height. LLR caps Limited Building licensees at Group 1, 2 or 3, so the largest job a Limited Building contractor can bid is $1,000,000. Contractors who need taller buildings or bigger jobs take the Unlimited Building (NASCLA) exam instead.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Limited Building technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
