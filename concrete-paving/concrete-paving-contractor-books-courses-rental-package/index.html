How does it fit the South Carolina Concrete Paving exam?

The Concrete Paving classification covers commercial concrete roads, parking areas, curbs and flatwork. Concrete paving candidates study slip-form and fixed-form paving, joints, finishing and the highway specifications used on public paving work.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Concrete Paving technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
