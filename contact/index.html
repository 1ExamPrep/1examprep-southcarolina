The contact form loads here. If it does not appear, call (877) 775-9400 or email sales@1examprep.com.

What should you include in your message?

  • The license or classification you are going for, for example Roofing or Residential Builder.
  • Your exam date, or how many weeks you have to study.
  • Whether you already own any of the reference books.
  • If you want help with the LLR application or forming an LLC.

With those 4 details we can usually recommend one package and tell you what it costs.

Need help with an order?

Include your order number and the email used at checkout. Books ship free by USPS Media Mail, which usually takes 2 to 8 business days. Online courses arrive by email after checkout, so check your spam folder before calling.

What can the team help you with?

Most calls fall into one of five areas. Bring any of them to the phone or put them in an email.

  • Choosing books. We match your classification to the PSI reference list, including the exact editions allowed in the testing room.
  • Tabs and highlighting. We explain what the pre-printed tabs cover and when a highlighted and tabbed set is worth the extra cost.
  • Courses and live classes. We compare the online course, the live virtual training and the rental packages for your schedule.
  • The LLR application. The License Application Service and the LLC setup take the paperwork off your hands.
  • Company orders. We quote books for several qualifiers or several classifications at once.

What happens after you get in touch?

A member of the 1 Exam Prep team reads every message and replies by phone or email, whichever you prefer. For product questions we usually send a short list: the package we recommend, its price and what is in the box. When you are ready, the Buy now button on any product page opens checkout with that item already in the cart. Licensing questions about eligibility or experience are decided by LLR, so we point you to the right board page when that is the real question. Exam scheduling is handled by PSI. You can see both boards' official pages in the sources list below.
