Are South Carolina contractor exams open book?
Yes. PSI allows the approved reference books in the testing room for the CLB and Residential Builders Commission exams, so tabbed and highlighted books are worth having.
Do I need approval before scheduling the exam?
Not for Contractor's Licensing Board exams: you schedule with PSI directly and apply after you pass. Residential Builders Commission candidates apply first and wait for the Exam Eligibility letter.
