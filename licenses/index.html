How do you know which license you need?

Start with the work and its value. Commercial work in a regulated classification over $10,000 needs a CLB license. Homes need an RBC license. Individual electricians and plumbers need a local card. The licensing guide walks through each path step by step.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions South Carolina candidates ask us most, checked against LLR and PSI on November 24, 2026.

How many South Carolina contractor licenses are there?
The CLB lists dozens of classifications and subclassifications. This site covers 33 of them, including every one we stock prep material for.
What is the difference between CLB and RBC?
The Contractor's Licensing Board licenses commercial general and mechanical contractors. The Residential Builders Commission licenses residential builders and residential specialty contractors.
Can I hold more than one classification?
Yes. A CLB license can carry several classifications. You pass the technical exam for each one and the Business and Law exam once.
Is there a statewide electrician license?
Not for individual journeymen. South Carolina electrician cards are local, while electrical contracting businesses hold a CLB or RBC license.
Do all licenses need the Business and Law exam?
Every CLB and RBC license needs the matching Business Management and Law exam. Local electrician and plumber cards do not.
Where do I schedule the exams?
PSI runs the South Carolina contractor exams. CLB candidates schedule directly with PSI; RBC candidates wait for the Exam Eligibility letter first.
