What does the South Carolina Residential Builder license cover?

A Residential Builder license from the Residential Builders Commission covers building and remodeling homes in South Carolina. The technical exam can be waived for a current SC General Contractor or for anyone who passed the NASCLA exam in the past 3 years, but the Business Management and Law exam still applies.

Which exams do Residential Builder candidates take?

PSI gives every South Carolina contractor exam at its test centers. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current LLR pages and PSI bulletins.

ExamWhat it coversFormat

Business Management and Law for Residential BuildersScale score of 68 to passOpen book

How do you get the Residential Builder license?

  1. Apply to the Residential Builders Commission first and pay the application fee.
  2. Wait for the Exam Eligibility letter by email or mail; eligibility lasts 1 year.
  3. Schedule both exams with PSI and pay PSI directly.
  4. Pass the trade exam and Business Management and Law (scale score 68) within the 12 months.
  5. Send proof of insurance or bonding as the Commission asks, then receive the license.
