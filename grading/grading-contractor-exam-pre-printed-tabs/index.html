How does it fit the South Carolina Grading exam?

The Grading classification covers commercial earthwork, excavation, site grading and erosion control. Grading questions focus on earthwork quantities, compaction, trench safety and the 811 utility locate rules that apply to every excavation in South Carolina.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Grading technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
