What is in the NASCLA Residential Electrician - Online Exam Prep?

The South Carolina NASCLA Residential Electrician - Online Exam Prep is designed for electrical professionals pursuing the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractor examination as an approved technical-exam waiver pathway toward a South Carolina Residential Electrician license. The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractor examination. Applicants who have passed the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractors exam within the previous three years may use that examination result to waive the technical portion of South Carolina's Residential Electrician examination requirements. The NASCLA pathway does not eliminate South Carolina's other licensing requirements. Applicants must still satisfy the Commission's work-experience requirements, complete the licensing application, and pass the South Carolina Business Management and Law examination. The Residential Builders Commission determines whether an applicant satisfies all requirements before issuing the Residential Electrician license. Together, these references support study across residential wiring, theory, safety, calculations, project management, fire alarm systems, residential construction, and professional contracting topics.

How does it fit the South Carolina Residential Electrical exam?

A Residential Electrical license from the Residential Builders Commission covers electrical work on homes. South Carolina has exam waiver agreements for the residential electrical technical exam with North Carolina and Mississippi, and the NASCLA electrical exam is also accepted. The Business Management and Law exam is still required.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Residential Electrical exam: Trade knowledge and the residential code (Open book)
  • Business Management and Law for Residential Builders: Scale score of 68 to pass (Open book)

What will you study with it?

Exam Details The NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program includes three electrical trade examinations: Electrical Contractor or Master Electrician, Journeyman Electrician, and Residential Electrical Contractor. NASCLA developed these examinations to provide participating licensing agencies with standardized trade examinations addressing electrical knowledge and practical trade responsibilities. The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission accepts the Residential Electrical Contractor examination. NASCLA examination scores must be within three years for South Carolina acceptance. Passing NASCLA waives the technical portion; it does not waive Business Management and Law. For comparison, applicants who use South Carolina's standard examination pathway take a Residential Electrical technical examination containing 60 multiple-choice questions and must obtain a passing score of 73.3%. The Business Management and Law examination contains 50 multiple-choice questions.
