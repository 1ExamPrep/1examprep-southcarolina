How does it fit the South Carolina Plumbing exam?

The Plumbing classification covers commercial plumbing, drainage, venting and water distribution. The commercial plumbing exam is built on the International Plumbing Code, and candidates who tab and highlight their code book finish with time to spare.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Plumbing technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
