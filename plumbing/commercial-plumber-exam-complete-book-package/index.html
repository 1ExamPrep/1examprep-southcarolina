How does it fit the South Carolina Plumbing exam?

The Plumbing classification covers commercial plumbing, drainage, venting and water distribution. The commercial plumbing exam is built on the International Plumbing Code, and candidates who tab and highlight their code book finish with time to spare.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Plumbing technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)

What will you study with it?

A complete book package supports this process by giving candidates access to the core materials in one convenient collection. The package includes the 2021 editions of the International Plumbing Code and International Fuel Gas Code, along with Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters by Lee Smith and the South Carolina 811 Excavator Manual. Together, these books address essential subjects such as plumbing fixtures, drainage, venting, water distribution, fuel gas piping, appliance installation, measurement, formulas, pipe calculations, excavation safety, and damage prevention. These references are useful both during exam preparation and as ongoing professional resources.
