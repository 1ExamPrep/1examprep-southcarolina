What is in the Commercial Plumber - Online Exam Prep Course?

The South Carolina Commercial Plumber - Online Exam Prep Course provides course access only. The required or recommended reference books are not included with this product and must be obtained separately. The goal of the course is to help candidates prepare to pass the contractor exam through organized study guidance, trade-focused review, practice-oriented learning, reference-book navigation, and confidence-building preparation. The course does not replace the official books. Instead, it helps students use the books more effectively by connecting exam topics with the references where those topics are addressed. Open-book contractor exams require more than owning the correct books. Candidates must recognize the subject of a question, decide which reference applies, locate the relevant chapter or table, understand the information presented, and select the correct answer within the available testing time. This online course is structured to support that process. How does it fit the South Carolina Plumbing exam?

The Plumbing classification covers commercial plumbing, drainage, venting and water distribution. The commercial plumbing exam is built on the International Plumbing Code, and candidates who tab and highlight their code book finish with time to spare.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Plumbing technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)

What will you study with it?

The course helps organize those subjects into manageable study areas so students can focus on one part of the exam at a time while building familiarity with the complete reference set. This course is appropriate for experienced plumbing professionals, qualifying parties, contractors, supervisors, estimators, and tradespeople who are preparing for the South Carolina Commercial Plumber Contractor exam.
