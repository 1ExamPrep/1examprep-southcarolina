Which South Carolina board licenses you?

South Carolina splits contractor licensing between two boards inside the Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation (LLR). The Contractor's Licensing Board (CLB) covers commercial general and mechanical contractors, alarm businesses and boiler work. The Residential Builders Commission (RBC) covers homes. Electrician and plumber cards for individual workers are handled locally.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions South Carolina candidates ask us most, checked against LLR and PSI on November 24, 2026.

Do I need a contractor license in South Carolina?
You need a CLB general or mechanical contractor license for regulated commercial work over $10,000, and a Residential Builders Commission license for residential building and specialty trade work.
Are South Carolina contractor exams open book?
Yes. PSI runs the CLB and RBC trade exams and both Business and Law exams as open-book tests with the approved references.
How many questions are on the Commercial Business and Law exam?
The Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors exam has 50 questions and a 125-minute limit. You need 35 correct answers to pass.
What score passes the Residential Business and Law exam?
The Residential Builders Commission requires a scale score of 68 on the Business Management and Law exam.
Can I skip the Residential Builder trade exam?
Yes, if you hold a current SC General Contractor license or passed the NASCLA exam in the last 3 years. The Business and Law exam still applies.
How long does a CLB license last?
General contractor licenses expire October 31 in even-numbered years and mechanical contractor licenses on October 31 in odd-numbered years.
