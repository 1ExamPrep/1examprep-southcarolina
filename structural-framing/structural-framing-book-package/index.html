How does it fit the South Carolina Structural Framing exam?

The Structural Framing classification covers commercial framing in wood, light-gauge steel and structural steel. Framing candidates study load paths, connections, bracing and erection safety.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Structural Framing technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)

What will you study with it?

Code of Standard Practice for Steel Buildings and Bridges, 2022 Modern Welding, 11th Edition, 2013 Manual of Steel Construction, 15th Edition
