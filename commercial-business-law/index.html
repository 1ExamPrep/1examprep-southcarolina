What does the South Carolina Commercial Business and Law license cover?

The Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors exam is required for every CLB general and mechanical license, and for alarm and pool licenses. The exam has 50 questions, a 125-minute limit and needs 35 correct answers to pass. It is open book, based on the NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, South Carolina edition.
