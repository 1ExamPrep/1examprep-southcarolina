How does it fit the South Carolina Swimming Pools exam?

The Swimming Pools classification covers commercial and residential pool construction, equipment and repair. Pool contractors pass the PSI Swimming Pools exam and the Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors exam, and there is no pre-approval needed to schedule them. South Carolina Regulation 61-51 for public pools is part of the study list.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Swimming Pools technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)

What will you study with it?

Each reference addresses a different part of the knowledge required to plan, build, equip, operate, and maintain swimming pools and related systems. Preparation is not limited to memorizing isolated facts. Candidates should understand how pool construction subjects connect in the field. A pool shell may involve soil preparation, excavation, reinforcing steel, concrete materials, shotcrete placement, curing, and structural details. Equipment installation may involve circulation, filtration, sanitation, electrical bonding, grounding, wiring methods, and code-required clearances. Public swimming pool projects may also involve state-specific permitting, design, safety, water-quality, and operational requirements. The South Carolina Swimming Pool Contractor Online Exam Prep Course provides an organized framework for reviewing these connected topics. The goal is to help candidates approach the examination with stronger trade knowledge, improved reference-navigation skills, and a more consistent study routine.
