How does it fit the South Carolina Fire Alarm exam?

A Fire Alarm Contractor license lets a business design, install, service and maintain fire alarm systems in South Carolina. Under the Alarm System Business Act, the qualifying employee passes the PSI Fire Alarm exam and the Alarm Code of Laws exam, and the application needs an NTS Level I or II or a NICET Level II or III certificate, as applicable.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Fire Alarm exam: NFPA 72 and fire alarm practice (Open book)
  • Alarm Code of Laws exam: SC Alarm System Business Act (Open book)
