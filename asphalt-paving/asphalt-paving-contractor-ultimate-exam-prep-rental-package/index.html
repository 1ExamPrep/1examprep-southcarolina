How does it fit the South Carolina Asphalt Paving exam?

The Asphalt Paving classification covers commercial asphalt surfacing, base preparation and related grading for roads, lots and drives. Highway and paving candidates study plant mix, compaction, base courses and estimating, plus the SCDOT highway specifications that many paving jobs are bid against.

Candidates for this license sit 2 exams:

  • PSI Asphalt Paving technical exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Books allowed (open book))
  • Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors: 50 questions, 125 minutes, 35 correct to pass (Open book)
