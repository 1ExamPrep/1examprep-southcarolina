What does the South Carolina Journeyman Electrician license cover?

Journeyman electrician cards in South Carolina are issued by cities and counties, which usually require a PSI or ICC exam based on the National Electrical Code. Check with your local building department for the code edition and exam it uses. The study guides here come in 2023 NEC and 2026 NEC editions.

Which exams do Journeyman Electrician candidates take?

Local credentials use exams chosen by the city or county. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current LLR pages and PSI bulletins.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
Journeyman Electrician exam (local)National Electrical Code lookups and calculationsUsually open book

Who issues the license in South Carolina?

South Carolina does not issue a statewide journeyman electrician card. Cities and counties do, and most use a PSI or ICC exam built on a recent code edition. Ask the building department where you work which edition it tests, then pick the matching study guide. The 15 packages here include current and older editions, clearly labelled.
