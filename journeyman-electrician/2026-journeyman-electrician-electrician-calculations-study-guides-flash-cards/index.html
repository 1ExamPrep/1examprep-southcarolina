How does it fit the South Carolina Journeyman Electrician exam?

Journeyman electrician cards in South Carolina are issued by cities and counties, which usually require a PSI or ICC exam based on the National Electrical Code. Check with your local building department for the code edition and exam it uses. The study guides here come in 2023 NEC and 2026 NEC editions.

Candidates for this license sit 1 exam:

  • Journeyman Electrician exam (local): National Electrical Code lookups and calculations (Usually open book)

What will you study with it?

The South Carolina Journeyman Electrician Study Guide helps organize journeyman-level electrical topics into a practical study path. The Electrician Calculations Study Guide gives focused support for Ohm’s Law, formulas, load-related questions, conductor sizing, motor concepts, transformer concepts, voltage drop, and other math-based subjects. Journeyman electrician preparation requires more than field experience alone. Field work helps candidates understand real installations, but an exam requires careful reading, topic recognition, reference navigation, calculations, and time management.
