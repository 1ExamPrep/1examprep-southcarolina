What does the South Carolina NASCLA Commercial General Building license cover?

The NASCLA Commercial General Building exam is the path to an Unlimited Building (UB) license, which lets a South Carolina general contractor build commercial structures of any height. South Carolina accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building in place of its own building exam, and a passing NASCLA score also waives the technical part of the Residential Builder exam if it is less than 3 years old.

On a CLB license the classification shows as a 2-letter code followed by a group number, so BD3 means NASCLA Commercial General Building work up to $1,000,000 per job. A license is needed once regulated commercial work costs more than $10,000.

How much work can you bid with the license?

South Carolina general contractor licenses come in groups set by your financial statement or surety bond. The group caps the value of any single job or bid. The initial license fee is $350, and general licenses expire on October 31 of even-numbered years.

GroupLimit per job
Group 1$100,000
Group 2$400,000
Group 3$1,000,000
Group 4$3,000,000
Group 5Unlimited
